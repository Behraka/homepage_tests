The process

A California dissolution,start to judgment.

The sequence, and the clocks that do not move for anyone.

Day 0Filing and service

Petition filed and served FL-100 / 110

Automatic restraining orders take effect. Property transfers, beneficiary changes, insurance cancellations and out of state travel with the children are all restrained from that moment.

Day 30Hard deadline

Response due FL-120

Thirty calendar days from personal service. Silence is not neutral. It permits a default judgment entered without you.

Week 2 to 8If it cannot wait

Temporary orders FL-300

Support, a custody schedule, exclusive use of the residence, and a contribution toward attorney fees. The first hearing sets the terms everything after is measured against.

OngoingMandatory

Declarations of disclosure FL-140 / 142 / 150

Every asset, debt and income source, community and separate alike. Incomplete disclosure is the most common reason a judgment is set aside years later.

Month 3 to 12Where value moves

Discovery, valuation and experts

Business valuation, tracing of separate property, equity and deferred compensation, Watts and Epstein credits, and where warranted a forensic accountant.

Six monthsFamily Code § 2339

Judgment and termination of status

California will not end a marriage sooner than six months and one day after the respondent was served. A statutory floor, not an estimate, and it runs from service rather than filing.

An uncontested matter can finish close to that floor. A contested one involving a business or a custody dispute commonly runs twelve to twenty four months in this county.

Questions

Answered plainly,before you call.

How long does a divorce take in California?
Six months and one day from the date the respondent was served, at the earliest. That is a statutory floor under Family Code § 2339, not a typical timeline. Uncontested matters often finish near it. Contested Los Angeles County matters involving a business or a custody dispute commonly run twelve to twenty four months.
Is California really a fifty fifty state?
Community property is divided equally, but the argument is almost never about the split. It is about what counts as community property to begin with. Property acquired before marriage, by gift or by inheritance is separate, and separate funds mixed into community assets can often be traced back out.
Will I have to appear in court?
Most clients appear once, or not at all. The large majority of California dissolutions resolve by written settlement. Appearances become likely where temporary orders are contested, custody is disputed, or a party will not disclose.
How is spousal support calculated?
Temporary support during the case is normally set with a guideline calculator run on both incomes and the timeshare with the children. Long term support after judgment is different: the court weighs the factors at Family Code § 4320, including the marital standard of living and the length of the marriage. Guideline output carries no weight at that stage.
What does this cost?
An initial retainer against an hourly rate, set by the complexity of the matter. You will be told the figure in the first conversation, before you engage. Where one spouse controls the finances, a request for the other side to contribute toward attorney fees is often available early.
I was served today. What comes first?
Note the date and count thirty days, because that is your deadline. Do not move money, change beneficiaries or cancel insurance, all of which are already restrained. Then call. A response can be prepared quickly, and an extension can often be negotiated.
